Why EventHub?

WHY CHOOSE US?

Everything you need to discover, register, and participate in college events — all in one place.

🎟️
Easy Registration

Register for any event in seconds. Fill your details once, get a PDF ticket with a unique QR code instantly — no queues, no paperwork.

📜
Auto Certificates

Download a professional participation certificate for every event you attend — automatically generated with your name, event details, and a unique ID.

🔔
Real-Time Updates

Get instant notifications when new events are announced or admin posts announcements. Never miss an important update again.

🏆
Leaderboard & Rankings

Compete with fellow students on the participation leaderboard. The more events you attend, the higher your rank among your peers.

📊
Personal Dashboard

Track your event history, view participation stats with charts, manage your wishlist, and download all your tickets and certificates from one place.

🤖
AI Assistant

Our built-in AI chatbot is available 24/7 to answer your questions about events, registration, tickets, and anything else about the platform.
